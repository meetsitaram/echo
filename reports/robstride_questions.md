# Questions for RobStride

Draft, not sent. To be sent by the user when the time is right; until then the design uses the conservative assumptions in `actuator_interfaces.md`.

1. What is the output bearing type on RS00, RS02, RS03, RS04 and RS06?
2. For each, what are the allowed axial load, radial load and tilting moment at the output, static and dynamic, and where is the radial load measured from?
3. Are hub thread depths as drawn? RS04 9-M5 6.5 min, RS06 6-M4 6, RS02 6-M4 7 min: the STEP shows 3.5, 4.7 and 6.0.
4. What screw grade and tightening torque do you recommend for each hub and stator thread? Is thread locker allowed? What is the hub and housing material?
5. Is there any hollow-shaft version, or an approved way to route cables past the joint?
6. RS04: is the 7.3 mm outlet at the tab a fixed cable, what is its length, and may it exit elsewhere? What are the two silicone notes for?
7. May the rear threads be used for mounting: RS03 and RS06 rear ears, RS04 rear M4, RS02 rear through-holes? With what load limit?
8. RS04: are the 2 x 5 mm features on the 106 circle pins or holes, and what fit do they need?
9. What flatness, run-out and register fit do you specify for the hub and for the mating part?
10. What do the side bumps on RS03 and RS06 contain, and can we build close to them?
11. Do the rated torques need the heat sink plate on a particular face, and may a screwed structural part replace it?
