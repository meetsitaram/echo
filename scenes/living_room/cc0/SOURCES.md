# Textures: Poly Haven, CC0

Every image here is a diffuse (colour) map at 1K from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain: free to use, change and publish, no
credit required). Downloaded 2026-11-01 from `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/<name>/<name>_diff_1k.jpg`.

`tools/scene_living_room.py` keeps each photo's detail and recolours it to the room in the clip (`recolour`), writing
the PNGs MuJoCo reads to `scenes/living_room/build/` (not in the repo).
